# Performance and Bonus

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October, on a 1-5 rating scale. Promotions are decided in the April cycle.

Annual bonus target is 10% of base salary for levels L1-L3 and 15% for L4 and above. A rating of 3 or higher is required to qualify for the bonus.

A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasts 60 days.
