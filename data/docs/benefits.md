# Benefits

Health insurance covers the employee, spouse and up to 2 children with a sum insured of ₹5,00,000. Parents can be added at the employee's own cost. An annual health check-up is provided.

Gym membership is reimbursed up to ₹2,000 per month. Provident fund contribution is 12% of basic pay.

Referral bonus: ₹30,000, paid after the referred employee completes 6 months at the company.
