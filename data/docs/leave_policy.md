# Leave Policy

Annual leave: every employee gets 24 days of annual leave per year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next financial year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March.

Sick leave: employees get 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

Casual leave: 6 days per year. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave.

Parental leave: maternity leave is 26 weeks. Paternity leave is 10 working days.
