# Travel and Expense Policy

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are mandatory for any single expense above ₹500.

Meals: the daily meal limit is ₹1,500 for domestic travel and ₹3,000 for international travel.

Flights: economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours; premium economy for flights longer than 6 hours.

Hotels: the cap is ₹8,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) and ₹5,000 per night elsewhere.

Approvals: managers can approve claims up to ₹50,000. Claims above ₹50,000 need approval from the Finance Head.
