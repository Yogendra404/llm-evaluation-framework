# Remote and Hybrid Work

Zentra Labs follows a hybrid model. Employees must be in the office 3 days a week: Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Core working hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST.

Working remotely from outside your city of employment is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year and needs prior manager approval.

New joiners receive a one-time home-office stipend of ₹15,000. The company also reimburses internet costs of ₹1,000 per month on submission of the bill.
